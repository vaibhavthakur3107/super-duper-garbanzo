        ~ A Poem by THE ONE ~

  Each verse below describes one dancer's pose.
  The first letter of each verse reveals which letter it shows.
  Arms point like clock hands: UP is noon, DOWN is six,
  LEFT is nine, RIGHT is three - diagonal fills the mix.
  Legs spread outward from the center line they know,
  And body tilts left negative, right positive in flow.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Ascending left arm reaches to the SKY, right arm points below,
   The legs stand firm together, upright with graceful flow.

Balanced stance with left arm pointing RIGHT, while right hangs DOWN,
   One leg lifts up gently bent, the dancer holds their ground.

Crossing paths the arms do take - left reaches DOWN and LEFT,
   While right arm sweeps UP diagonal, with legs spread, nothing bereft.

Dramatically both arms stretch OUTWARD, LEFT and RIGHT they fly,
   Horizontal like a crossbeam, legs together standing by.

Eagerly the left arm LOW diagonal, while right arm UP does soar,
   The legs spread wide like victory, an X shape to explore.

Firm the left arm points to SKY, while right slopes DOWN and RIGHT,
   The legs together standing straight, a simple pose upright.

Gracefully both arms go diagonal - left DOWN-LEFT, right UP-RIGHT,
   The legs spread outward gently, the body holds its height.

Halfway right the left arm drops to rest, arm falls to SIX,
   While right arm stretches SIDEWAYS out, left leg bends in the mix.

In this pose the left hangs DOWN, the right reaches UP to noon,
   Both legs spread apart in stance, dancing to silent tune.

Joyfully both arms reach UP, forming a V so wide,
   Diagonal toward the heavens, legs spread at either side.

Keen the pose: left arm UP-LEFT, right arm DOWN-RIGHT diagonal,
   The legs cross close together, stance somewhat optional.

Left arm stretches SIDEWAYS out, pointing to NINE on the clock,
   The other arm stays hidden low, legs together like a rock.

Mirrored not from L at all - left arm UP, right arm SIDEWAYS RIGHT,
   One leg bent while spread apart, an asymmetric sight.

Now both arms hang LOW diagonal - DOWN-LEFT and DOWN-RIGHT they fall,
   The legs spread very WIDE apart, the widest pose of all.

One arm hangs at DOWN-LEFT diagonal, the other straight DOWN below,
   The legs angle to one side, a casual pose to show.

Pointing UP the left arm climbs to DIAGONAL high,
   While right arm stretches to the SKY, legs spread as dancers fly.

Quite reversed: left arm to SKY, right arm UP-RIGHT diagonal,
   The legs stand close together, a pose more classical.

Rising left arm points to SKY, right arm slopes DOWN diagonally,
   The legs together standing straight, posed very naturally.

Slanting body tilts to RIGHT, left arm DOWN-LEFT diagonal,
   Right arm UP-RIGHT reaching high, legs spread - pose is jovial.

Two arms raised form a V, both pointing UP toward the sky,
   The legs stand firm together, standing straight and high.

Under left arm hangs at DOWN, right arm UP-RIGHT diagonal,
   Both legs spread apart in stance, the pose is rational.

Vigorously left arm LOW diagonal, right arm UP to noon,
   Body tilts with legs spread wide, dancing to the tune.

Waving left arm UP-LEFT diagonal, right arm DOWN-RIGHT in mirror,
   The legs spread out quite WIDELY, making the pose clearer.

eXcited pose with left UP-LEFT, right arm UP to SKY,
   The body tilts leftward slightly, legs spread wide nearby.

Yearning left arm UP to SKY, right UP-RIGHT diagonal soars,
   The legs stand close together, a gentle pose explores.

Zero motion: both arms DOWN, hanging loose at either side,
   Legs together, body straight, in stillness dancers bide.
